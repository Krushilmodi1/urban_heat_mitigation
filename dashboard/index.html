Executive Research Summary

This research formulates an urban heat island mitigation framework for the semi-arid metropolitan region of Ahmedabad, Gujarat. Using a 1 km × 1 km spatial discretization, the project incorporates a simplified 2D thermal PDE regularizer into neural networks to model surface skin cooling sensitivity (ΔLST) and near-surface air cooling estimates (ΔT_air).

Key Analytical Takeaways:
  • Spatial Generalization: Evaluating on contiguous 8 km blocks prevents spatial adjacency leakage and provides a rigorous out-of-sample evaluation, revealing a substantial generalization gap for tree ensembles under spatial block holdouts (test R² drops to 0.034 despite high training fit).
  • PINN Regularization: The physics loss (λ_pde = 0.1) acts as a numerical regularizer, reducing the formulated 2D thermal PDE residual MSE by 22.2% (from 0.962 down to 0.748) while maintaining 0.32 °C MAE.
  • Canopy Decoupling: Pedestrian 2m air cooling is attenuated relative to radiometric rooftop skin cooling (ΔT_air ≈ 0.32 × ΔLST).
  • Spatial Prioritization: Multi-Criteria UHVI balances thermal hazard (40%), structural footprint (40%), and vegetative deficit (20%) using empirical rank percentiles.

Physics-Informed Regularization Deep-Dive

Thermal Advection-Diffusion PDE Regularizer (Simplified Diffusion-Reaction Formulation):

The PINN optimizes a composite loss combining empirical data fidelity with a simplified thermal PDE regularization residual:

L_total = L_data + λ_pde · L_pde   (λ_pde = 0.1)

The steady-state 2D diffusion-reaction thermal balance operator penalizes non-physical spatial gradient discontinuities:

f_pde = α·∇²T + S(NDVI, NDBI, density) - β·T = 0
where ∇²T = ∂²T/∂x² + ∂²T/∂y²,   S = γ_bld·density + γ_ndbi·NDBI + γ_ndvi·NDVI

Why the PINN has lower R² (0.014 vs 0.056 for Ridge): The physics loss acts strictly as a numerical regularizer. It penalizes non-physical spatial gradient spikes, trading off empirical training-set curve-fitting flexibility to reduce the formulated thermal PDE residual MSE from 0.962 down to 0.748 (22.2% reduction).

Note: A 22.2% reduction in the implemented PDE residual reflects numerical regularization under the formulated 2D steady-state heat equation; it does not establish thermodynamic conservation, prove a physical heat-transfer model, or demonstrate real-world cooling efficacy without empirical microclimate ground truth.

Residual Spatial Autocorrelation (Moran's I):

-0.006 (p = 0.44)

A residual Moran's I = -0.006 (p = 0.44) fails to reject the null hypothesis of spatial randomness under inverse-distance weighting at this scale. While this demonstrates that the contiguous 8 km spatial block holdout successfully avoided naive spatial autocorrelation leakage, it does not constitute proof of absolute spatial independence across all spatial processes or scales.

Synthetic Target Variation & Modest Test R² Context:

Total target variation across the 780 spatial holdout cells is narrow (standard deviation σ = 0.407 °C, Var(LST) = 0.166 °C²). In the offline test generator (tests/synthetic_data.py), an independent Gaussian perturbation with nominal σ = 0.35 °C was added to cell temperatures. Combined with the low prototype building coverage (mean 0.08%) and the strict 8 km spatial block split evaluating models on unseen geographic regimes, this high noise-to-signal ratio limits explainable out-of-sample variance across all model architectures (test R² = 0.01 – 0.06), while absolute errors remain small (RMSE ≈ 0.40 °C, MAE ≈ 0.32 °C).

Mathematical Formulations

1. Urban Heat Vulnerability Index (UHVI):

UHVI_i = 0.40·RankPct(LST_i) + 0.40·RankPct(ρ_bld,i) + 0.20·RankPct(1 - NDVI_i)

Empirical rank percentiles (empirical CDF ranks scaled to [0, 1]) are computed across all cells to prevent extreme right-skewed building density outliers from distorting vulnerability prioritization.

2. Non-Linear Cooling Saturation:

ΔLST_comb = 6.5 · [1 - exp(-ΣΔLST_i / (6.5 · 0.85))]

3. Air Temperature Coupling:

ΔT_air ≈ 0.32 · ΔLST (Canopy boundary layer)

Known Research Limitations
  • Earth Engine Authentication: Live USGS Landsat rasters require interactive browser OAuth (earthengine authenticate). In offline mode, the pipeline operates on the calibrated synthetic proxy raster.
  • Building Sample Extent: D:\395_buildings.csv contains 4,777 buildings inside the study area, resulting in 0.08% mean density. Real city-scale applications require uncompressed municipal building datasets.
  • Physics Regularization vs. Ground Truth: The 22.2% reduction in the implemented PDE residual indicates compliance with the formulated 2D heat energy operator; it does not substitute for empirical satellite ground-truth validation.
  • Synthetic Counterfactuals: Modeled cooling values represent parameter sensitivity projections under the prototype data-generating process, not empirically measured Ahmedabad microclimate cooling.
  • Microscale CFD: The 1 km grid resolution averages building canyon geometry. Localized street-level turbulence requires microscale modeling (ENVI-met).

🌙 Nighttime Satellite Observations Unavailable
Scientific Integrity Protocol: Nighttime thermal observations cannot be synthesized, modeled, or proxy-extrapolated from daytime observations without fabricating scientific results.

The existing project data pipeline is based strictly on USGS Landsat 8 and Landsat 9 Collection 2 Level-2 (sensors OLI/TIRS). Landsat operates in a sun-synchronous daytime orbit passing over Ahmedabad at approximately 10:30 AM local solar time. Consequently, all observed LST and radiometric indices (NDVI, NDBI) in this repository are daytime observations.

Required Datasets to Support Nighttime UHI Analysis:
  • Nocturnal Satellite Products: MODIS Terra (MOD11A2, ~10:30 PM local time), MODIS Aqua (MYD11A2, ~1:30 AM local time), VIIRS nocturnal products (VNP21), or ECOSTRESS nighttime acquisitions.
  • Processed Rasters: Grid rasters (e.g. grid_landsat_night_2024_1000m.tif or grid_modis_night_1000m.tif) aligned to the 1,000 m UTM 43N grid.
  • Tabular Columns: Dedicated nighttime surface temperature variables (e.g. LST_night) joined into data/processed/spatial_grid_metrics.csv.
